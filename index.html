The dossier

It started in a hotel kitchen

Hisham grew up in America chasing one particular thing: the thick, stuffed New York cookie you queue around a block for. Back home in Panchgani, running the family's Hotel Ravine, he could not find it anywhere in India. So he started baking it himself.

Lockdown turned that into an obsession — dozens of recipes, dozens of techniques, one cookie that finally got it right. Friends fell for the texture first. Word spread. Requests turned into orders, orders turned into a waitlist, and the waitlist turned into Cookie Cartel.

Today the operation runs out of a bakery in Kandivali West, Mumbai, still baking every single cookie fresh on order and shipping it to doorsteps across the country.

— El Jefe
