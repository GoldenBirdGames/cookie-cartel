The confession

Chasing a cookie he could not buy

Hisham grew up in America on thick, stuffed New York cookies — the sort with a crisp shell, a soft middle and something molten hidden in the centre. Back in Panchgani, helping run the family's Hotel Ravine, there was simply nowhere in India to get one.

So he started baking. Lockdown gave him the time and the hotel kitchen gave him the oven, and what followed was dozens of recipes, dozens of failures and one batch that finally landed.

Friends fell for it first. Word travelled faster than he expected, requests turned into orders, and by the time the kitchen could not keep up, Cookie Cartel had a name, a look and a line of people waiting.

— El Jefe
